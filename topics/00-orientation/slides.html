What this course is

Using Big Data to Solve Economic and Social Problems

  • The course is built around broad topics, not chapters — each one has material to read, watch, and explore
  • Material goes up at least two weeks before we cover it
  • Every class starts with a printed guided notes handout: it tells you what to focus on and gives you room to write
  • Your grade: exams 60%, projects 7.5%, in-class project quizzes 22.5%, attendance 5%, participation 5%

Setting up Posit Cloud (one time)

Do this once, in week 1. It takes about two minutes.

  1. On the course home page, click Join Posit Cloud.
  2. Create a free account — use your OSU email (signing in with your OSU Google account is easiest).
  3. You land in the ECON 1101 workspace: open 0.getting-started first.
  4. Projects run in RStudio in your browser — nothing to install.

Prefer your own computer? Each project has a downloadable template zip on the home page.

Joining TopHat (one time)

Attendance and in-class participation are 5% + 5% of your grade, and both run through TopHat.

  1. Click TopHat in the course menu, or use the join link on the home page.
  2. Choose Log in with school account and pick Ohio State — your regular OSU sign-in.
  3. Enter the join code from the home page if asked. It is posted on the course home page and I will read it out in class; it is deliberately not printed in these slides.
  4. Attendance and participation run through TopHat every class — bring a charged device, and set it up before the first graded question.

You can miss up to 4 classes with no penalty, so you do not need to email me when you will miss one. If TopHat gives you trouble, Ohio State’s set-up guide is linked on the course home page.
